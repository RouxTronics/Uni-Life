# Overview 

Snell's law is a formula used to describe how light or other waves bend when passing from one transparent medium into another

![[Pasted image 20261003192945.png]]

![[Pasted image 20261003192930.png]]

- **n₁ and n₂**: The refractive index (a measure of how much a substance slows down light) of the first and second medium.
- **θ₁ (angle of incidence)**: The angle of the incoming wave measured from the normal line (an imaginary line perpendicular to the surface).
- **θ₂ (angle of refraction)**: The angle of the outgoing, bent wave also measured from the normal line.

## How It Works

**Bending Toward the Normal**: When light enters a denser material where it moves slower (n₂ > n₁, like going from air into water), it bends closer to the normal line
