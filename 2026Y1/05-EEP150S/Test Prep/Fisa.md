## Question 1 

If the temperature increases at a rate of 3°K per second, how many hours must elapse to increase the temperature 100°K ?

>[!INFO]
>Temp Rate : 3°K /s
>Temperature Change: 100°K
>Hours: ?

$$ t = \frac{\Delta T}{TemperatureRate}(s)$$

$$=\frac{100}{3}=33.333s$$

- convert from seconds to hours 

$$=\frac{33.333}{(60\times60)}$$ 
$= 9.25\times{10}^{-3} hours$

> **Answer**: $9.25\times{10}^{-3} hours$
## Question 2 

When a force of 12N is applied to a mass of 200kg. Calculate the acceleration in $m/s^2$

>[!INFO]
>Force(F): 12 N
>Mass(m): 200 kg
>Acceleration(a): ?

$$F = ma$$
$$a = \frac{F}{m}$$

$$a = \frac{12}{200}$$

$$a =0.06\space m/s^2$$

## Question 3

A dam with the following average dimensions 1 km long, 2 km wide and 20 m deep is situated at
an average height of 1000 m above sea level. Calculate the volume of the dam.

$$\begin{aligned}
V &= long \times wide \times \text{deep} \\
&= (1\times 10^3)(2\times 10^3)(20) \\
&= 40 \times 10 ^6 m^3

\end{aligned}$$
