## Calculation Problems 

### Question 3

Calculate the force required to lift  94.5 kg ? (**Answer Example**: 5.2N)


>[!INFO]
>
Force (weight) to lift $m = 94.5\,\text{kg}$ 
$g = 9.81 m/s²$



$$
\begin{aligned}
F &= m \times g  \space(N)\\
&= 94.5 \times 9.81 \\
&= 926.1\,\text{N} \\
\end{aligned}
$$
> **Answer** 927.045 N

### Question 9

Calculate the work done in raising a 83 kg elevator to a height of 66 meter from ground level. (**Answer Example:** 5.2KJ)


Work done raising $m = 83\,\text{kg}$ to $h = 66\,\text{m}$ $$ W = mgh = 83 \times 9.81 \times 66 \approx 53684.4\,\text{J} = 53.7\,\text{kJ} $$ **Answer:** **53.7kJ**
### Question 10

If the temperature increases at a rate of 0.041°K per second, how many hours must elapse to increase the temperature 100°K ? (**Answer Example:** 5.27 hours or 5.27 hrs)


Temperature increase $100\,\text{K}$ at $0.041\,\text{K/s}$ $$ t = \frac{100}{0.041} \approx 2439\,\text{s} \quad \to \quad \frac{2439}{3600} \approx 0.678\,\text{hours} $$ **Answer:** 0.678 hrs
### Question 11

When a force of 480.4N is applied to a mass of 200kg. Calculate the acceleration in $m/s^2$
(**Answer Example:** 5.22 m per second sq)


Acceleration when $F = 480.4\,\text{N}$ on $m = 200\,\text{kg}$ $$ a = \frac{F}{m} = \frac{480.4}{200} = 2.402\,\text{m/s}^2 $$ **Answer:** 2.402 m per second sq



### Question 17

Calculate the force, in kN, required to give a mass of 3,202kg and acceleration of $4\,\text{m/s}^2$
> (**Answer Example:** 422kN)

Force in kN to accelerate $m = 3202\,\text{kg}$ at $a = 4\,\text{m/s}^2$ $$ F = ma = 3202 \times 4 = 12808\,\text{N} = 12.808\,\text{kN} $$ **Answer:** 12.808kN
### Question 19

A dam with the following average dimensions 96 km long, 7.4 km wide and 27 m deep is situated at an average height of 86 m above sea level. Calculate the potential energy of the water.
(**Answer Example:** 1.519e+15 J) Round your answer to 3 decimal places.


Potential energy of water: $96\,\text{km} \times 7.4\,\text{km} \times 27\,\text{m}$ at $h = 86\,\text{m}$ 

$V = (96\times10^3) \times (7.4\times10^3) \times 27$ = $$1.91808\times10^{10}\,\text{m}^3$$

$m = 1000 \times V =$ $m = 1000 \times 1.91808\times10^{10}\,\text{m}^3 =$  $$1.91808\times10^{13}\,\text{kg}$$ 

$PE = mgh = 1.91808\times10^{13} \times 9.8 \times 86 \approx$ $$1.617\times10^{16}\,\text{J}$$

**Answer:** 1.617e+16 J

### Question 34

A dam with the following average dimensions 69 km long, 1.8 km wide and 8.1 m deep is situated at an average height of 117 m above sea level. Calculate the mass of the water in the dam.
(**Answer Example:** 1.519e+15 kg) Round your answer to 3 decimal places.

> [!INFO]
> long: $69 \times 10^{3} \space m$
> wide: $1.8 \times 10^{3} \space m$
> deep: $8.1 \space m$



Mass of water in dam: $69\,\text{km} \times 1.8\,\text{km} \times 8.1\,\text{m}$ 

$$
\begin{aligned}
V &= length \times width \times depth \space(m^3) \\
&= (69 \times 10^3) \times (1.8 \times 10^3) \times 8.1\\
&= 1.00602 \times 10^9\,\text{m}^3
\end{aligned}
$$

- $1\space m^3\space \text{water} =1000\space kg$
$$
\begin{aligned}
m &=  \rho V \space(kg) \\
&= 1000 \times (1.00602 \times 10^9)\\
&= 1.00602 \times 10^{12}\,\text{kg}
\end{aligned}
$$


**Answer:** 1.006e+12 kg
