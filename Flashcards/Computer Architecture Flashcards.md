- What is Moore's Law >> `Moore’s law describes the trend that processing power doubles every 2 years, due to the doubling of transistors`

- What is [[Dennard scaling]]>> `Dennard scaling is a principle stating that as transistors get smaller, their power density stays constant because voltage and current drop in proportion to their size`

- Why is [[Moore’s Law]] no longer observed>>`Moore’s Law ended because physical limits and rising costs make it nearly impossible to keep shrinking transistors on a silicon microchip`

- Why is [[Dennard scaling]] no longer observed
?
- `It ended because shrinking transistors further caused severe current leakage and extreme heat generation that could no longer be safely cooled`
- `Also voltage could not be decreased further while still maintaining the dependability of the integrated circuits.`
